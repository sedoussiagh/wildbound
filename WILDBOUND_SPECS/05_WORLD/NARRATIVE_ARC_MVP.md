# MVP Narrative Arc

## Act I — A Voice in the Roots

The hero awakens near `Oakheart Village` with a Heartroot seed reacting to their
memory. `Elder Rowan` teaches that being Wildbound is an agreement, not a title.
The tutorial battle against `Moss Slime` shows corruption without cruelty.

## Act II — Whispers in Moss

In `Whispering Woods`, spores repeat phrases from villagers. The player follows
traces, helps another adventurer and sees `Brambleback` defend a Fissure rather
than cause it. Defeating it releases a fragment of `Wild Mirror`.

## Act III — The Shape We Choose

At level 10, the mirror shows two valid futures. The player chooses an evolution
after interactive previews. No branch is framed as good/evil. A first branch
change is offered later so experimentation is safe.

## Act IV — Roots Remember

`Moonroot Hollow` contains memories removed from their owners. The group learns
terrain and pack tactics. Phantom scenes never alter controls or impersonate a
real player.

## Act V — The Empty Antlers

`The Hollow Stag` tries to protect itself by erasing painful memories. After its
defeat it is freed, not killed. Its empty antlers project the outline of the
`Hollow Crown`, establishing the next region.

## Narrative acceptance criteria

- Main arc is fully soloable.
- Dialogue has skip, history and replay summary.
- No choice of evolution changes morality or locks narrative rewards.
- Every boss motive is readable before or immediately after battle.
- A session interruption resumes at the latest completed dialogue beat.
