# Whispers in Moss

Main quest, minimum level 2, estimated 8 minutes.

## Steps

Enter `Whispering Woods`; defeat three `Moss Slime`; collect three `Moss Gel`.
Combat drops acquired during the quest count. Collection does not consume gel.

## Rewards

50 `Paw Coins`; one `Moss Lamp`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Party kills credit all present active members.
- Existing Moss Gel before activation does not count; newly acquired does.
- Lamp overflow enters reserve and quest still completes.
