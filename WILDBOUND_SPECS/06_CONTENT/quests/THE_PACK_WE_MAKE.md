# The Pack We Make

Main quest, minimum level 4, estimated 8 minutes.

## Objective

Complete one party combat in `Whispering Woods`. Matchmaking may pair with a
mentor. If no human partner is found after 45 seconds, the player may use a named
NPC helper; the UI never pretends it is a player.

## Rewards

60 `Paw Coins`; one `Softleaf Rug`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Solo alternative appears without shaming.
- AFK presence alone does not grant credit.
- The quest introduces invite, quick phrase, block and leave controls.
