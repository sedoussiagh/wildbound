# Onboarding

## First-session target

| Minute | Outcome |
|---:|---|
| 0–2 | consent, account and class choice |
| 2–4 | appearance and name |
| 4–7 | guided Moss Slime battle |
| 7–10 | Oakheart Village and permanent charm |
| 10–15 | first free exploration |
| 15–20 | den or optional social interaction |

## Flow

1. Neutral age/consent screen appropriate to region.
2. Guest authentication; account linking proposed after first victory, not before.
3. Compare three classes with role, difficulty and both future evolutions.
4. Customize color, eyes and pattern.
5. Name validation.
6. `A Seed of Memory` interactive tutorial.
7. Enter `Oakheart Village`; show one objective, not all menus at once.
8. Choose first expressive reward: `Rowan Cape`, `Moss Headband` or future lamp.

## Combat teaching beats

Camera/grid → select hero → show legal movement → preview and confirm basic skill
→ explain AP/MP → enemy intention → defensive or positional skill → end turn →
victory receipt. An incorrect tap is explained and recoverable, never punished.

## Skip and replay

Returning accounts can skip non-safety steps. Future heroes skip account consent
and may skip battle. Tutorial is replayable without rewards from Settings.

## Acceptance criteria

- No screen contains more than one mandatory primary action.
- Class selection remains reversible until final hero creation.
- Future evolution previews do not promise post-MVP forms as available.
- Closing the app resumes at the last confirmed step.
- Onboarding can complete with quick chat disabled and without social contact.
