# Roots Remember

Main quest, minimum level 12, estimated 12 minutes.

## Steps

Enter `Moonroot Hollow`; inspect first memory panel; defeat two
`Crystal Beetle`; reach `Stag Gate`.

## Reward

100 `Paw Coins`; unlock permanent dungeon checkpoint explanation, not the
checkpoint itself outside a session.

## Acceptance criteria

- Solo encounter spawns sequential beetles if simultaneous pair exceeds budget.
- Credit survives room transition and reconnect.
- Existing past kills do not count retroactively.
